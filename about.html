About LA H 513

LA H 513 Soccer Club is focused on helping young soccer players improve their skills and become more confident on and off the field.

We believe soccer is about more than just winning. It is also about teamwork, hard work, respect, and having fun.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help players become better soccer players while creating a positive and supportive environment.

What We Focus On

We focus on player development, teamwork, confidence, and learning the game of soccer.

Why Soccer Matters

Soccer gives young players an opportunity to stay active, make friends, learn responsibility, and work toward their goals.
