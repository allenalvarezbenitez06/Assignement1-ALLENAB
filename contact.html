About the CEO

My name is Allen, and I am the CEO of LA H 513 Soccer Club. I created LA H 513 because I have a strong passion for soccer and helping young players improve.

I have experience coaching young soccer players and working with different age groups. My goal is to help players develop their skills, confidence, teamwork, and love for the game.

My Goals for LA H 513

I want LA H 513 to be a place where players can learn, work hard, have fun, and become better every day. I also want to create a positive environment where players feel supported on and off the field.

As the club continues to grow, I hope to help more players reach their goals through soccer.
